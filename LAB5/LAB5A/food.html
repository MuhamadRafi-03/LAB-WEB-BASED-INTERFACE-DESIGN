About the Festival

This festival showcase the terengganu food culture

Beyond street food tasting, the festival features live cooking demonstrations, traditional kuih-making workshops, and acoustic cultural performances throughout the weekend. Bring your family and friends to experience the best dishes the region in venue.

Free Entry for everyone! Don't forget to join it
