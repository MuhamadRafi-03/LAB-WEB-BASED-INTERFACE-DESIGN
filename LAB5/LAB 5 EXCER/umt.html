About UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It was officially established on 1 February 2007. The university is primarily focused on marine science and aquatic resources, reflecting its coastal location.

UMT's history can be traced back to the establishment of the Fisheries and Marine Science Centre in 1979. Over the years, it evolved through several iterations before finally being upgraded to a full-fledged university. Today, it offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs across various faculties.
